Wordle: Guess the Hidden Five Letter Word

Wordle is a word puzzle in which you try to guess a hidden five letter word within six attempts. After each guess, the letters change colour to show which ones are correct and in the right place, which ones are in the word but misplaced, and which are not in the word at all.

How to Play Wordle

You type a valid five letter word and use the colour feedback to narrow down the possibilities. Good starting words use common letters, and each guess should teach you something. It is a pleasant balance of vocabulary and logic. This is an unofficial browser version.

Tips for Better Results

  • Start with a word that contains several common vowels and consonants.
  • Use your later guesses to test letters you have not tried yet.
  • Think about which letter combinations are possible in English.

Who Will Enjoy It

A quiet, thoughtful game that fits a few minutes of calm. The original game belongs to its owners.
