Draw the Hill: Sketch a Path and Let the Vehicle Roll

Draw the Hill is a creative physics game where you draw the ground your vehicle needs to drive on. Instead of controlling the car directly, you design the terrain, which makes each level a puzzle about slopes, bridges and momentum.

How to Play Draw the Hill

You use the mouse to draw hills, ramps and bridges, then set the vehicle in motion and watch what happens. If it gets stuck or falls off, you can adjust the lines and try again. Gentle slopes and smooth curves often work better than steep drawings.

Tips for Better Results

  • Keep your hills gentle so the vehicle does not lose speed.
  • Test often; small changes can make a big difference.
  • Use bridges to cross gaps and keep the path continuous.

Who Will Enjoy It

It is an imaginative game that rewards experiments, great for players who enjoy creative problem solving.
