Territorial: Grow Your Land in a Battle for the Map

Territorial is a real time strategy game where you expand your territory and attack neighbours to take over the map. Your land generates troops, and the way you spend them determines whether you grow or fall to a stronger rival.

How to Play Territorial

You click to claim empty land and launch attacks at enemy territory. Controlling the balance between expanding and defending is the key. Alliances and timing play a large role in multiplayer matches.

Tips for Better Results

  • Expand into empty land early to build your economy.
  • Attack weaker neighbours instead of strong ones.
  • Keep enough troops on borders to defend.

Who Will Enjoy It

A deep strategy game with simple controls, great for fans of conquest.
